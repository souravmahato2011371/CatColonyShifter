Prism Pop Privacy Policy

Effective date: 20 November 2026

Prism Pop is an offline match-three puzzle game. This policy explains how the app handles information.

Information collection

Prism Pop does not ask you to create an account and does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information. The app contains no advertising SDK, analytics SDK, location tracking, or online multiplayer service.

Information stored on your device

Game progress, best score, level, and sound preferences are stored locally in the app's private storage on your Android device. They are used only to remember your game settings and progress. The app disables Android app-data backup for this local save. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage removes this locally stored information.

Permissions and third parties

The app does not request sensitive device permissions and does not send information to a developer-operated server. No third-party data processors are integrated into the current version.

Children and families

Prism Pop does not collect personal information from players. The app's target-audience and content-rating declarations are provided separately in Google Play Console and must accurately reflect the final distribution and marketing of the app.

Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change, this policy will be updated before the relevant app update is distributed.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the project maintainer through the Prism Pop project issue page.
