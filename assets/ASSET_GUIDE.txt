CAT COLONY: SHIFTER — ASSET DROP ZONES

You can attach assets in chat or add them to these folders. The game keeps procedural stand-ins until an asset is integrated.

MODELS
- assets/models/characters/shifter.glb — player character (preferred: GLB)
- assets/models/worlds/verdant/verdant.glb — Verdant environment pieces
- assets/models/creatures/ — friendly creatures and colony visitors
- Prefer GLB for one-file models. Keep mobile meshes lightweight and include textures in the GLB where possible.

TEXTURES
- assets/textures/worlds/verdant/ — ground, foliage, rocks and sky materials
- assets/textures/characters/ — fur, markings and accessories
- PNG or WebP are preferred. Keep large textures near 1024–2048 px unless detail genuinely needs more.

ANIMATION
- assets/animations/ — animation clips or supporting files. GLB can also carry its own animation clips.

AUDIO
- assets/audio/music/verdant.ogg — original/licensed ambience or music
- assets/audio/sfx/ — collection, jump, gate, building and UI sounds
- OGG is preferred for web delivery; MP3 is an alternative.

UI AND FONTS
- assets/ui/ — SVG or PNG icons, emblems and illustrations
- assets/fonts/ — WOFF2 font files with suitable web-use rights

Please provide original assets or files you have permission to use. Tell me what each asset is intended to represent when sharing it.
